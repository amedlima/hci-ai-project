## 2. Planted errors in detail

### S1-E1: claim 2 (Wrong date or client detail)

- **AI statement:** The company employs 31 staff and is owned by its two directors, Eleanor Harlow (60%) and Marcus Finch (40%).
- **What is wrong:** States 31 employees.
- **Correct information:** The company currently employs 34 staff. 31 was last year's figure. The ownership part of the claim is correct.
- **Evidence:** D1 §1 Business details, “Number of employees”: 34; D3 §1 Client snapshot at last review, “Number of employees”: 31
- **Classification:** Factual error (Incorrect client information)
- **Severity:** Low
- **Why it matters:** The agent copied the prior-year figure. A small error, but it shows the summary mixing up current and previous records.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Uncertain, Medium confidence
- **Corrected statement:** The company employs 34 staff and is owned by its two directors, Eleanor Harlow (60%) and Marcus Finch (40%).
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S1-E2: claim 5 (Wrong year-on-year comparison)

- **AI statement:** Operating profit also increased, to £174,000 from £162,000 the previous year.
- **What is wrong:** States the previous-year operating profit as £162,000, so reports an increase.
- **Correct information:** Previous-year operating profit was £182,000. Operating profit fell by £8,000 (4.4%) to £174,000.
- **Evidence:** D2 §1 Profit and loss summary, “Operating profit”: £174,000; D3 §2 Financial figures, year ended 31 December 2024, “Operating profit”: £182,000
- **Classification:** Incorrect comparison (Incorrect year-on-year comparison)
- **Severity:** High
- **Why it matters:** Reverses the direction of the profit trend, which is central to a decision to lend more.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Discrepancy, Low confidence
- **Corrected statement:** Operating profit fell to £174,000 from £182,000 the previous year.
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S1-E3: claim 8 (Wrong figure)

- **AI statement:** The facility is secured by a debenture over the company's assets and a personal guarantee from Eleanor Harlow limited to £57,000.
- **What is wrong:** States the personal guarantee limit as £57,000.
- **Correct information:** The personal guarantee from Eleanor Harlow is limited to £75,000.

- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S1-E4: claim 11 (Omission of a material change)

- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

## 4. Scoring

- **Error found (hit):** the participant marks an incorrect claim as wrong (flags, edits or rejects it). Count it even if their correction is not exactly right; record correction accuracy separately.
- **Missed error (over-trust):** an incorrect claim left unmarked or approved.
- **False rejection (under-trust):** a correct claim marked as wrong.
- Detection rate = hits ÷ 5. False-rejection rate = false rejections ÷ 7.
- For the omission, accept any answer that identifies the missing item, even if the participant attaches it to a different claim or says it in the final comment.
