## 2. What is held constant across the three test sets

- Same four document types with the same section layout and the same row labels, so a fact is always in the same place.
- 12 claims per summary: 7 correct, 5 incorrect.
- The same five error slots: one wrong figure (FIG), one wrong date or client detail (DET), one wrong year-on-year comparison (CMP), one conclusion not supported by the evidence (CON), one omission of a material change (OMI).
- The same cue pattern in Conditions B and C: CMP is marked Discrepancy; DET, CON and OMI are marked Uncertain; FIG is marked Supported with High confidence (a planted over-trust trap); one correct covenant claim is marked Uncertain (a planted false alarm). So 4 of 5 errors carry a warning cue and 4 of 5 warning cues sit on errors.
- CMP always needs the current-year statement (D2) and last year's record (D3). OMI always needs the client declarations (D1 §4) and last year's snapshot (D3 §1).
- Every figure quoted in a summary appears in the documents as written; the only arithmetic is the revenue percentage change, which is correct in all sets.
- Year-on-year errors (for UR-06): CMP and OMI in every set, so two per set.

## 4. Assessment

The sets are closely matched in length, structure, error mix, cue pattern and the documents needed to verify each error. They are not identical in difficulty: the positions of errors differ by design, severity differs slightly, and the Set 1 conclusion error is easier to spot without the documents than those in Sets 2 and 3.

These differences are the reason the set-to-condition pairing is rotated (see Part C). With rotation, any difference between sets is spread evenly over the three conditions instead of favouring one.

Recommended check: after the pilot, compare detection rates by set (pooled over conditions). If one set is clearly easier or harder, report it as a limitation; do not change the materials after the main study has started.

## 5. Limits of this assessment

Difficulty has been matched by construction, not measured with people. Only a pilot can confirm it. The 5-minute target in UR-09 has not been timed; a 12-claim summary across four documents may take 5 to 8 minutes in Condition A.
