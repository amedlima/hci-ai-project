## 2. Planted errors in detail

### S3-E1: claim 3 (Wrong figure)

- **AI statement:** The company is requesting an increase in its overdraft limit from £120,000 to £160,000 to fund seasonal stock purchases.
- **What is wrong:** States the requested overdraft limit as £160,000.
- **Correct information:** The requested limit is £180,000, an increase of £60,000.
- **Evidence:** D1 §3 Facility request, “Requested facility”: Overdraft, limit £180,000; D1 §3 Facility request, “Increase requested”: £60,000 (50.0%)
- **Classification:** Factual error (Incorrect financial amount)
- **Severity:** High
- **Why it matters:** The amount requested is the core of the decision. An approver relying on the summary would consider the wrong figure.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Supported, High confidence
- **Corrected statement:** The company is requesting an increase in its overdraft limit from £120,000 to £180,000 to fund seasonal stock purchases.
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S3-E2: claim 6 (Wrong year-on-year comparison)

- **AI statement:** Gross margin improved to 44.0%, from 43.0% the previous year.
- **What is wrong:** States the previous-year gross margin as 43.0%, so reports an improvement.
- **Correct information:** Previous-year gross margin was 45.0%. Gross margin fell by one percentage point to 44.0%.
- **Evidence:** D2 §3 Key ratios, “Gross margin”: 44.0%; D3 §2 Financial figures, year ended 31 March 2025, “Gross margin”: 45.0%
- **Classification:** Incorrect comparison (Incorrect year-on-year comparison)
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Why it matters:** Reverses the direction of the margin trend; the business is earning less on each sale, not more.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Discrepancy, Low confidence
- **Corrected statement:** Gross margin fell to 44.0%, from 45.0% the previous year.
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S3-E3: claim 8 (Wrong date or client detail)

- **AI statement:** The facility is secured by a debenture over the company's assets and a personal guarantee from Daniel Reyes limited to £60,000.
- **What is wrong:** Names Daniel Reyes as the guarantor.
- **Correct information:** The personal guarantee is given by Helen Ashcombe. The £60,000 limit and the debenture are correct.
- **Evidence:** D4 §2 Security, “Personal guarantee”: Helen Ashcombe, limited to £60,000
- **Classification:** Factual error (Incorrect client information)
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Why it matters:** Names the wrong person as legally liable for the guarantee.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Uncertain, Medium confidence
- **Corrected statement:** The facility is secured by a debenture over the company's assets and a personal guarantee from Helen Ashcombe limited to £60,000.
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S3-E4: claim 10 (Omission of a material change)

- **AI statement:** Compared with last year's review, the differences flagged are higher stock, higher bank borrowing and no overdraft excesses in the past 12 months (one in the previous review period). No other material changes were identified.
- **What is wrong:** Says no other material changes were identified. The differences it lists are correct.
- **Correct information:** The client declared a county court claim for £41,500 issued on 14 April 2026 by a former supplier, Fernbrook Nurseries. Last year's review recorded no legal actions.
- **Evidence:** D1 §4 Client declarations, “Legal actions or court claims?”: Yes. A county court claim for £41,500 was issued against the company on 14 April 2026 by a former supplier, Fernbrook Nurseries. The claim is being defended; no hearing date has been set.; D3 §1 Client snapshot at last review, “Legal actions or court claims”: None
- **Classification:** Omission (Missing important information)
- **Severity:** High
- **Why it matters:** A pending court claim is a potential liability and a change from last year. This is exactly what the agent was asked to flag.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Uncertain, Medium confidence
- **Corrected statement:** Compared with last year's review, the differences flagged are higher stock, higher bank borrowing, no overdraft excesses in the past 12 months (one in the previous review period), and a new county court claim for £41,500 from a former supplier.
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

### S3-E5: claim 11 (Conclusion not supported by the evidence)

- **AI statement:** Stock rose to £402,000 from £318,000 while revenue grew 5.0%, which indicates that stock is selling through more quickly than last year.
- **What is wrong:** Concludes that stock is selling through more quickly. The two stock figures quoted are correct.
- **Correct information:** Stock growing much faster than sales means stock is selling more slowly. Stock days increased from 75 to 89.
- **Evidence:** D2 §3 Key ratios, “Stock days”: 89; D3 §2 Financial figures, year ended 31 March 2025, “Stock days”: 75
- **Classification:** Unsupported claim (Incorrect conclusion)
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Why it matters:** Turns a warning sign about slow-moving stock into a positive, when the request is to fund more stock.
- **Cue shown in Conditions B and C:** Uncertain, Low confidence
- **Corrected statement:** Stock rose to £402,000 from £318,000 while revenue grew 5.0%; stock days lengthened from 75 to 89, so stock is selling through more slowly than last year.
- **Expected reviewer action:** Edit (correct it) or Reject

## 4. Scoring

- **Error found (hit):** the participant marks an incorrect claim as wrong (flags, edits or rejects it). Count it even if their correction is not exactly right; record correction accuracy separately.
- **Missed error (over-trust):** an incorrect claim left unmarked or approved.
- **False rejection (under-trust):** a correct claim marked as wrong.
- Detection rate = hits ÷ 5. False-rejection rate = false rejections ÷ 7.
- For the omission, accept any answer that identifies the missing item, even if the participant attaches it to a different claim or says it in the final comment.
